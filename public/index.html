What is RubyConf Thailand?

Southeast Asia's only event where you'll network with Rails core contributors, CTOs, and engineers shipping code at scale.

You will network with Rails core contributors and CTOs from Asia's fastest-scaling startups, absorb production-tested strategies from engineers who've shipped code at Evil Martians and Circle, and walk away with solutions to problems you're dealing with right now.

Speakers will take the stage and present real-world lessons on Domain Driven Design after 15 years of Rails, operating Rails post-deployment, ethical AI implementation, cell-based architecture, and simulation testing for background jobs.

After soaking up the knowledge, look out for some extra perks at the end of each day: the official party with food and drinks, networking sessions over Bangkok's skyline.

RubyConf Thailand 2026 is a two-day conference event taking place on January 31st and February 1st in Bangkok.
